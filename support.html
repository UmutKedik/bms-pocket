Support

Questions, problems or a controller that does not behave? Write to umut.kedik1@gmail.com. Please include the app version (Settings › Version), the protocol and, if you can, the controller maker and model.

Subscriptions

  • Cancel or change plan: iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › BMS Pocket, or tap the tier badge (top right) › Manage subscription in the app.
  • New phone or reinstall: open the tier badge (top right) and tap Restore purchases.
  • Refunds: Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Common questions

  • No devices found: pick the network interface the controllers are on before scanning, and allow Local Network access in iPhone Settings › BMS Pocket.
  • A command stayed on a device: open the Command tab and release it there.
