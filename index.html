BMS Pocket: building controls in your pocket

Scan once and see every controller on the network. BMS Pocket finds BACnet, Modbus, OPC UA and Niagara (oBIX) devices in one scan and shows the protocol of each device. Then browse its points, read live values and send commands from your iPhone. No laptop, no account, no cloud.

For BMS technicians, commissioning engineers, system integrators and facility teams.

Download on the App Store

Why BMS Pocket

  • One scan, four protocols: choose the protocols, scan once and get one device list
  • Each device shows its protocol and address, and its vendor and model when the device reports them
  • Browse devices and points, search them and add them to your watchlist
  • Read, write, override and release, with the BACnet priority array and read-back
  • Modbus device types and Niagara (oBIX) stations that you save stay on your iPhone

Four protocols in one app

  • BACnet/IP: scan, devices behind routers, BBMD, points with their priority array.
  • Modbus TCP: read registers by hand, or use device types that you can edit and save.
  • OPC UA: read and write variables on servers that accept anonymous access without encryption.
  • Niagara over oBIX: sign in to a station that has the oBIX service, read and command its points.

From the first scan to the report

  • Discover: find the controllers on the network, or add one by IP address.
  • Monitor: live values, watchlist, BACnet weekly schedules.
  • Command: confirm, choose the priority, and the app reads the value back. All commands held by the app are in one list, and you can release them at any time.
  • Diagnose: alarms, overrides and devices that do not answer, on one page.
  • Commission: a point-by-point checkout with a PDF report. EDE, OPC UA tag list and CSV exports.

Plans

Basic is free: scan all four protocols and read BACnet points and Modbus registers, up to 100 devices a day. Pro adds OPC UA, Modbus device types, commands, schedule editing and exports. Engineer adds Niagara over oBIX, command history and the PDF commissioning report.

Your data stays on your phone

The app works on the local network. No account, no cloud, no analytics, no data collected. See the Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

Which devices does BMS Pocket find?

Choose BACnet/IP, Modbus TCP, OPC UA and Niagara (oBIX), then scan once. The app lists every device it finds with its protocol and address, and its vendor and model when the device reports them.

Do I need a laptop or a gateway?

No. Connect the iPhone to the same network as the controllers, for example over Wi-Fi, and scan.

Can I write values and release overrides?

Yes, with Pro or Engineer. The app asks you to confirm before the first command to each device. For BACnet you choose the priority, and the app reads the value back. All commands held by the app are in one list. Releasing a command is free in every plan.

Does the app collect my data?

No. There is no account, no cloud and no analytics. What the app saves stays on your iPhone.

What does it cost, and which iPhones can use it?

Basic is free. Pro and Engineer are subscriptions, and the App Store shows their prices. The app needs iOS 15 or later.
