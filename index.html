BMS Pocket

A field tool for building automation technicians: discover, read, command and commission BACnet/IP, Modbus TCP, OPC UA and Niagara controllers from an iPhone.

  • Works on the local network. No account, no cloud.
  • Commands are confirmed, tracked and released.
  • Commissioning checkouts with a PDF report, EDE and tag list exports.
