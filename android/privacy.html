Privacy Policy

Draft for Android — review before release

Short version: BMS Pocket does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything the app records stays on your phone.

Who we are

BMS Pocket is published by Umut Kedik (the "developer"). For the purposes of the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK), the developer would be the data controller. Because the app collects no personal data, no processing takes place. Contact details are on the Support page.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-up or login with the developer.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No servers run by the developer: the app never sends data to the developer.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information, as defined by the CCPA/CPRA or any other law.

Data that stays on your phone

To do its job, the app keeps the following only in its own storage on your device:

  • Devices you found or added, their points and values.
  • Commands you sent, their release status and the command history (audit log).
  • Commissioning checkouts and the names you typed into them (for example "Tested by", customer and site).
  • Passwords for Niagara stations you saved, kept in the Android Keystore-backed storage on this device. Android backup and device transfer are disabled for the app.

Deleting the app deletes all of this. The developer has no copy and cannot restore it.

Network access

The app connects only to building controllers on the networks you choose (BACnet/IP, Modbus TCP, OPC UA, Niagara oBIX). The app does not send data from these devices anywhere else.

Files you export

CSV, EDE and PDF files are created only when you tap export. They go wherever you send them through the Android sharing options. What happens to them afterwards is up to you and the service you choose.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Google Play. The developer receives no payment details or Google Account identity. The app verifies signed purchase information on the device to unlock features. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase itself.

Children

BMS Pocket is a professional tool for building automation technicians. It is not directed at children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, KVKK, the CCPA/CPRA and similar laws you have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal data and to object to its processing. Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side. You control all data in the app and can delete it by deleting the app. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts to collect data, it will ask you first.
